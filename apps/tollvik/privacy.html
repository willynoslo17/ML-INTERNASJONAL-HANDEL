Tollvik privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how the Tollvik mobile app (iOS and Android), the Tollvik web version and purchases on mlinternasjonal.no handle personal data.

1. Who is responsible

MARTINEZ LOZANO INTERNASJONAL HANDEL (trading as ML Internasjonal Handel), sole proprietorship, organisation number 935 407 095, Oslo, Norway, is the data controller. Contact: kontakt@mlinternasjonal.no.

2. What we process

  • Text you type into an analysis. When the live AI service is available, this text is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our AI processing service so that a result can be generated and returned to you. If the service is unavailable, the analysis runs locally on your device and nothing is sent.
  • Technical data needed to deliver a request (for example IP address and time of the request), processed by our hosting and automation providers.
  • Purchase data (web only): if you subscribe on this website, Stripe processes your payment details. We receive your email address, the plan and the payment status, never your full card number.

Tollvik has no user accounts, contains no advertising, and uses no analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. The apps on the App Store and Google Play do not offer purchases.

3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Generating the analysis you request — performance of the service you ask for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Selling and administering subscriptions, and bookkeeping — contract and legal obligation (art. 6(1)(b) and (c)).
  • Keeping the service secure and working — legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)).

Your text is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to build a profile of you and not linked to your identity.

4. Who receives data (processors)

  • Workflow automation provider (Make) that relays your request to the AI model.
  • AI model provider that generates the result.
  • Stripe (payments on this website).
  • Cloudflare (hosting of this website).

Some providers may process data outside the EEA. In that case we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Contact us for the current list of providers.

5. How long we keep data

We do not keep your analysis text in our own database. Providers may keep technical logs for a limited time under their own policies. Purchase records are kept for 5 years as required by the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act.

6. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, or object to processing by emailing kontakt@mlinternasjonal.no. You may also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no).

7. Please be careful

Do not enter sensitive personal data, or confidential information you do not want processed by the AI service.

8. Children

Tollvik is a business and personal-finance tool intended for adults and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

We will update this page when the processing changes and show the date above.
