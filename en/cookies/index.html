1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. We also use local storage (localStorage) to remember your cookie choice and, on the first visit, a limited first-touch attribution for the contact form.

2. Cookies we use

  • Necessary: storage of cookie consent (ml-cookie-consent) in the browser (localStorage), so your choice is remembered and the banner is not shown repeatedly.
  • Necessary: first-touch attribution for the contact form (ml-lead-first-touch) in localStorage: UTM parameters, referring page, landing page and first-visit time. These are sent only if you later submit the form, so we can see the origin of the enquiry.
  • Optional (only if you accept): the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, identifier 1425772629175058, to measure visits to the site and contact-form submissions. It may set cookies such as _fbp. If you reject, this Pixel is not loaded.

3. Consent

On your first visit we show a notice where you can accept or reject optional cookies. Necessary cookies are used so the site can remember your choice. You can change your choice with the button below, or by clearing site data in your browser and reloading the page.
