Who we are

ML Internasjonal Handel facilitates trade between companies in different markets. We identify opportunities, connect parties, and coordinate operations through to delivery.

We work with clear processes and direct communication. The aim is to reduce friction, shorten timelines, and complete transactions that can be repeated.

Values

  • Delivery: what is agreed is executed.
  • Clarity: concrete information at every stage.
  • Continuity: relationships continue after the first transaction.
