OUR SERVICES

Five services for international growth.

From market assessment to coordinated delivery, we support Norwegian and international companies throughout the trade process.

  • 01

    International trade and market access

    Import, export and structured entry into Norway, Europe, Asia and Latin America.

  • 02

    Strategic sourcing and international procurement

    Supplier search, qualification, quote comparison and commercial coordination.

  • 03

    Customs, regulatory and legal coordination

    Support with documentation, customs requirements, product rules and qualified professionals.

  • 04

    Transport, delivery terms, document flow, freight partners and shipment follow-up.

  • 05

    Commercial representation and business connections

    We connect buyers, suppliers and partners for long-term trade relationships.
