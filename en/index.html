Services

Five tasks. One point of contact.

From the first conversation with the counterparty to the goods being on their way.

  1. Negotiation on behalf of the client

    We conduct the dialogue with the counterparty within the mandate you give.

  2. Sourcing of suppliers and buyers

    We look for suitable parties, worldwide, for the mandate at hand.

  3. Export and import management

    We coordinate the practical carrying-out of the trade between countries.

  4. Customs clearance through an authorized broker

    Customs handling goes through an authorized customs broker.

  5. Logistics coordination

    Sea, air and road, chosen for the goods, the route and the timing.
