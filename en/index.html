Services

What we offer

Global Sourcing & Procurement, Supplier Representation, Buyer & Supplier Matching, Import & Export Support, and International Business Development.

  • 01

    Global Sourcing & Procurement

    We identify and evaluate suppliers against technical specifications, volumes and commercial conditions. The result is a verified shortlist ready for negotiation.

  • 02

    Supplier Representation

    We represent suppliers in destination markets: we present the offer, handle inquiries and advance negotiations. The supplier retains control of price and terms.

  • 03

    Buyer & Supplier Matching

    We match specific buyer requirements with actual supplier capacity. We only proceed when there is alignment on product, volume, timing and terms.

  • 04

    Import & Export Support

    We coordinate documentation, compliance and logistics from end to end. The client receives clear updates and goods move according to plan.

  • 05

    International Business Development

    We structure entry into new markets: identify counterparts, prepare the approach and facilitate first agreements. The focus is on operations that can be sustained.

Sectors

Sectors

International trade in these sectors.

  • Agriculture and AgriTech

    We manage trade in agricultural products and associated equipment and technology. We cover sourcing, quality verification and logistics adapted to the product.

  • Fisheries, Aquaculture and FishTech

    We facilitate transactions in seafood, aquaculture inputs and related technology. Coordination includes cold chain and compliance requirements.

  • Mining

    We connect demand and supply of minerals, equipment and associated services. We coordinate documentation, delivery terms and specialised logistics.

  • Oil and Gas

    We support trade in energy commodities, equipment and sector services. Management covers sourcing, compliance and logistics suited to the product.

  • Renewable Energy

    We facilitate the purchase and delivery of equipment and technology for renewable projects. The process includes technical matching and delivery coordination according to schedule.

  • Electronic Innovation

    We manage sourcing and distribution of electronic components and devices. We verify technical specifications and coordinate shipments with quality control.

  • Industrial Equipment

    We coordinate the procurement and transport of industrial machinery and tools. We ensure specifications are met and delivery occurs within the agreed timeframe.

  • Construction and Steel

    We facilitate trade in steel products and construction materials. We coordinate volumes, quality controls and efficient transport.
