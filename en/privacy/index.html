1. Controller

The controller is MARTINEZ LOZANO INTERNASJONAL HANDEL (ENK). Contact: info@mlinternasjonal.no.

2. Data we collect

  • Contact details you send via the contact form or email (name, email, optional company name and message).

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Responding to enquiries — contract / legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6 (1) b and f).
  • Operating and securing the website — legitimate interests (Art. 6 (1) f).
  • Optional Meta Pixel — consent (Art. 6 (1) a).

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. If you accept optional cookies, visit-measurement data is sent to Meta Platforms through the Meta Pixel, as described in the cookie policy. Data may also be processed by technical providers required for hosting and contact-form delivery, under data-processing arrangements where required.

5. Retention

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and follow-up, normally up to 24 months unless longer retention is required by law or claims.

6. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and object where applicable. You may lodge a complaint with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no).

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including HTTPS and limited access.

We may update this policy. The current version is published on this page.
