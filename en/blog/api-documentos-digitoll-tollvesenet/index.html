Advance submission of documents by API to Tollvesenet

Introducción

Tollvesenet (the Norwegian customs) allows sending in advance the supporting documentation for the declaration via an API. According to the current official page, invoices, permits, freights, certificates of origin or bills of lading can be transmitted so that they are available before the goods arrive at the border. The customs itself describes the solution as a pilot.

This article explains the conceptual benefit. It does not present the API as mandatory nor as a service of ML Internasjonal Handel.

Qué permite la API de documentos

The API allows the unsolicited and advance submission of supporting documents, as well as subsequent submission if the customs requests them. The documents are linked to the declaration in the TVINN system.

Documentos que pueden enviarse

Among the examples cited by the customs are invoices, permits, freight invoices, certificates of origin and bills of lading. The solution accepts usual file formats. The advance submission makes the documentation available to the officials when they process the declaration.

Beneficio conceptual del envío anticipado

By having the documents available before arrival, the need for subsequent contacts for documentary controls is reduced and the risk of delays in processing decreases.

Disponibilidad antes de la frontera

The goods may be subject to Digitoll notification and to prior declaration or at the time of crossing. Having the supporting documentation already uploaded facilitates the coherence between what is declared and what is provided. It does not replace the accuracy of the master data nor the responsibility of the declarant.

Carácter de solución piloto

Tollvesenet indicates that the solution has been developed based on consultations with the sector and is in pilot phase to verify its performance. It recommends its use, especially to those who declare in Digitoll towards certain clearance units, but does not declare it mandatory.

Descargo

Informative text based on the information published by Tollvesenet (https://www.toll.no/en/corporate/import/api-for-submitting-documentation-for-declaration-in-advance). It does not present the API as mandatory nor as a product of ML Internasjonal Handel. Consult the official technical specifications for implementation details.

Is the API mandatory?
No. The customs describes it as a solution in pilot phase that it recommends using.
What documents can be sent?
Among others, invoices, permits, freights, certificates of origin or bills of lading.
Does it replace the declaration?
No. It complements the declaration by making the supporting documents available in advance.
