Sea, air or road: criteria for choosing the mode

Introducción

The ML Internasjonal Handel site mentions international logistics by sea, air and road. The choice of mode is not universal; it depends on the nature of the goods, the required deadline, the temperature conditions and the documents associated with each means. This article presents conceptual criteria, without quoting freights or declaring one mode superior to the others.

Norway forms part of the European Economic Area, but not of the customs union of the European Union. That circumstance affects all modes, including road transport.

General criteria of choice

Companies usually consider volume and weight, urgency, temperature sensitivity and the total cost of the chain (freight, insurance, storage and formalities).

Nature of the goods and deadline

Goods of large volume and lower urgency are frequently transported by sea. High-value, urgent or low-volume products may go by air. Road is used for continental distances or as the final or initial leg of a multimodal chain. The delivery deadline agreed in the contract and the chosen Incoterm influence the decision.

Cold chain and special conditions

Perishable products —such as avocado, blueberries or salmon— require temperature control. Each mode has distinct capacities of refrigerated containers, chambers and transit times. The transfer of risk according to the Incoterm and the continuity of the cold chain must remain clear between the parties and the carrier.

Documents specific to each mode

Each mode generates transport documents with specific functions.

Bill of lading, AWB and CMR

In maritime transport the bill of lading is habitually used. In air, the air waybill (Air Waybill). In international road, the CMR consignment note. These documents evidence the transport contract and, in some cases, possession of the goods. They must be consistent with the commercial invoice, the packing list and the customs declaration in description, quantities and parties.

Norway and the road crossing

Although the truck circulates through EU territory before entering Norway, the goods are subject to Norwegian customs formalities when crossing the border. Advance notification (Digitoll) and the corresponding declaration also apply in the road mode. The fact that Norway is outside the customs union implies that there is no free circulation of goods in the Community customs sense.

Descargo

Informative text. It does not recommend a transport mode nor quote freights. Concrete conditions depend on the goods, the contract and the rules in force. Consult carriers, customs representatives and official sources.

Is there a mode that is always preferable?
No. The choice depends on the goods, the deadline, the total cost and the agreed delivery conditions.
What document is used on road?
Habitually the CMR consignment note for international road transport.
Does the truck avoid controls when entering Norway from the EU?
No. Norway is outside the EU customs union, so the road crossing also implies customs formalities.
