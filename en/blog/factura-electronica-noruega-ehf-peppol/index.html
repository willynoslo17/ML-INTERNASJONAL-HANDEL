Electronic invoice in Norway: EHF and Peppol BIS 3.0

Introducción

On 29 September 2026 the Tax Directorate (Skattedirektoratet) adopted a regulation that modifies the Norwegian Accounting Regulation. It was published by the tax administration on 30 September 2026. The text sets the approved formats for the obligation to issue electronic invoices between accounting subjects from 1 January 2027.

This article summarizes the main elements according to the sources available in November 2026. It does not invent exemption thresholds nor replace consultation of the official texts.

Regulation adopted on 29 September 2026

The regulation complements the modifications of the Accounting Act. It establishes that subjects obliged to keep accounts must issue electronic invoices when they sell goods or services to other subjects with the same obligation.

Approved formats

The approved formats are EHF Fakturering, Peppol BIS Billing, EHF Selvfakturering and Peppol BIS Self-Billing, all in version 3.0 or later. A PDF does not meet the definition of structured invoice for whoever can receive electronic format.

Calendar of obligations

The obligation to issue begins on 1 January 2027. The obligation to receive is set for 1 January 2030. Between both dates, the obligation to issue applies in practice when the recipient is in a position to receive electronic invoices (registered in ELMA/Peppol).

Issuance from 2027 and reception from 2030

Until 31 December 2029 the parties may agree other structured electronic formats. From 1 January 2030 reception becomes obligatory for accounting subjects. Official sources and published summaries further indicate limited exemptions for certain very reduced volumes of business; any concrete figure must be verified in the text of the regulation and in the clarifications of Skatteetaten, since nuances and exceptions may exist.

Implications for trade operations

Companies that invoice trade or service operations from Norway to other Norwegian accounting subjects will have to adapt their systems to the EHF or Peppol BIS 3.0 (or later) formats when the obligation applies to them. The commercial invoice used in customs and the fiscal electronic invoice are distinct documents, although they must be consistent in the overlapping data (parties, description, quantities, value).

Descargo

Informative text based on the regulation adopted on 29 September 2026 and published on 30 September 2026, and on summaries available in November 2026. It does not constitute fiscal advice. Thresholds, exemptions and details must be verified in the official sources of Skatteetaten. The rules may be modified.

Does a PDF comply as electronic invoice?
No. A PDF does not meet the definition of structured invoice for whoever can receive electronic format.
From when is issuance obligatory?
From 1 January 2027, between accounting subjects and, until 2030, in practice when the recipient can receive it.
When will reception be obligatory?
The obligation to receive is set for 1 January 2030.
