EUDR: application dates 2026-2027 and digital due diligence

Introducción

Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 on deforestation-free products (EUDR) establishes rules for the placing on the EU market or the export from the EU of certain goods associated with deforestation and forest degradation. According to the official page of the European Commission, the application dates are 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators and traders (and for micro and small already covered by the Timber Regulation) and 30 June 2027 for the rest of micro and small.

This article describes the concept of digital due diligence and the published dates. It does not anticipate sanctions or expand the list of products beyond what the official sources indicate.

Fechas de aplicación según la Comisión Europea

30 de diciembre de 2026 y 30 de junio de 2027

The European Commission publishes on its site that the EUDR has been in force since 29 June 2023 and that the differentiated application dates are those indicated. Subsequent sources confirm that, after the postponements and revisions, these dates are maintained. For operators that were already subject to the Timber Regulation (EUTR), the December 2026 date also applies to the wood products previously covered.

The cut-off date for deforestation is 31 December 2020: the product must not come from land deforested or degraded after that date.

Productos cubiertos y diligencia debida

Declaración digital con geolocalización

The products mentioned habitually are cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soy, wood and the derivatives listed in Annex I of the regulation. Additional products are not included here.

Due diligence requires that the operator who places the product for the first time on the EU market or exports it demonstrates three elements: that it is deforestation-free, that it was produced in accordance with the legislation of the country of production and that it is covered by a digital declaration (or simplified declaration in certain cases) that includes essential information, among it the geolocation of the plot of harvest or production.

The declaration is submitted through the EUDR information system integrated in TRACES. The competent authorities of the Member States carry out risk-based controls, with a country benchmarking system (low, standard, high).

Relación con otros controles de 2026

The EUDR is added to other 2026 requirements, such as the updated phytosanitary controls or, where applicable, the CBAM for other goods. For exporters from Latin America (for example of cocoa, coffee or soy) or from Norway (wood or other covered products), documentary preparation includes traceability to the geographical origin. A shipment to Norway is not the same as a shipment to the EU: Norway is not a member of the EU customs union and applies its own regulation.

Descargo

Informative text. It does not constitute personalised legal, tax or customs advice. The exact obligations, the products of the annex and the deadlines must be checked in the official text of Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 and in the guides of the European Commission in force on the date of the operation.

When does the EUDR apply for large and medium operators?
According to the European Commission page, on 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators and traders, and also for micro and small already covered by the Timber Regulation.
And for the rest of micro and small?
On 30 June 2027, according to the same source.
Which products are mentioned?
Cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soy, wood and derivatives of Annex I. The list is not expanded.
What does due diligence imply?
Demonstrate that the product is deforestation-free (cut-off date 31 December 2020), produced legally and covered by a digital declaration that includes geolocation of the plot.
