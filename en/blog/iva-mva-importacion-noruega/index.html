VAT/MVA on importation to Norway

Introducción

The VAT (MVA) on the importation of goods to Norway is managed differently depending on whether the company is registered in the MVA register. This article summarizes the general rules according to the official pages of Skatteetaten and Tollvesenet. It does not constitute a practical case nor invents exemptions.

Quién declara el MVA de importación

Companies registered in the MVA register calculate the import VAT themselves and declare it in the mva-melding before Skatteetaten. They do not settle it before Tollvesenet at the time of clearance.

Empresas registradas y no registradas

Whoever is not registered in the MVA register declares and pays the import VAT in the import declaration (normally through the customs representative or the carrier). Registered companies use the corresponding codes in the mva-melding and may, if they have the right to deduction, offset the amount.

Cómo se calcula la base

According to Skatteetaten, the base is, as a general rule, the sum of the statistical value plus the duties and other levies.

Valor estadístico y casillas 46 y 47

The statistical value appears in box 46 of the customs declaration and is expressed in Norwegian kroner. The duties and other levies (for example, special taxes or research fee when applicable) appear in box 47. The sum of both constitutes the general base on which the MVA rate is applied. There are exceptions for certain cases (works of art, reimportations after repair, etc.) that must be verified in the regulations and in the MVA manual.

Tipos de MVA

The Importguide of Tollvesenet, updated on 7 August 2026, indicates that the Storting sets the rates annually. The ordinary rate is 25 % and that for food is 15 %. These rates may change. They are not applied automatically to all goods; some have zero rate or are exempt according to their nature and use.

Companies must verify the applicable rate for each good and document the calculation by declaration and by rate.

Descargo

Informative text based on Skatteetaten (page on calculation of import MVA) and Tollvesenet (Importguide updated on 7.8.2026). It does not constitute tax advice nor a personalized calculation. The rates and the rules may be modified. Consult the official sources and authorized professionals.

Is the import MVA always paid to Tollvesenet at clearance?
Companies registered in the MVA register calculate and declare it in the mva-melding before Skatteetaten. Whoever is not registered declares it in the import declaration.
What is the general base?
As a general rule, the statistical value (box 46) plus duties and other levies (box 47).
What are the rates?
According to the Importguide of Tollvesenet updated on 7.8.2026, 25 % ordinary and 15 % on food. The Storting sets them annually and they may change.
