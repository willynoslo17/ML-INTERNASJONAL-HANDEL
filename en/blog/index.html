Digitoll Norway: digital transport notification to customs

Explanation of the obligation of digital notification and disclosure of transport information under Digitoll from 15 September 2026, who is involved and why data quality is relevant.

Customs declaration Norway before the border in 2027

Clear distinction between the Digitoll stage of 15 September 2026 (transport data) and that of 1 March 2027 (declaration at the crossing and end of the direct transport scheme).

Authorised customs representative in Norway: functions and limits

Explanation of the role of the authorised customs representative, its difference with respect to the owner of the goods and the carrier, and its fit with the intermediation model described by ML Internasjonal Handel.

Norway in the EEA but outside the EU customs union

Explanation that Norway is an independent customs territory, the difference between EEA, customs union and Schengen, and the practical consequences for shipments from the EU.

Incoterms 2020: what they cover and what they do not cover

Explanation of the 11 terms of Incoterms 2020, their main function (point of delivery and transfer of risk) and their limits with respect to the contract and to tariffs.

Incoterms for perishables: CIP and DAP versus FOB or CIF

Correct use of Incoterms for perishable goods. FOB/CFR/CIF only for sea or navigable waterways; CIP and DAP in air and road; transfer of risk and cold chain.

Usual import and export documents in Norway

Categories of documents that usually accompany imports and exports to or from Norway, according to operational practices and possibilities of sending by API to Tolletaten.

Tariff classification and HS code: why it matters

What the Harmonized System is, why the goods number in the Norwegian tariff is relevant and how a classification error alters the treatment.

Preferential origin in the EFTA–Peru free trade agreement

Explanation of the free trade agreement between the EFTA States and Peru, its rules of origin and the difference with the EU–Peru agreement.

Quality of master data for customs: description, origin and value

Why the quality of master data (description, origin, value, HS code) is relevant for customs compliance, especially with Digitoll.

CBAM 2026: what it is and which goods it usually affects

The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism entered its definitive application period on 1 January 2026. The concept of declarant is described, the usual goods according to sources and the Norwegian debate, without asserting identical application or giving certificate figures.

EUDR: application dates 2026-2027 and digital due diligence

The EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) sets differentiated application dates for large/medium operators and micro/small. The due diligence requirement (deforestation-free, legality and digital declaration) is explained without expanding the list of products or anticipating sanctions.

ICS2: when a security notification applies

ICS2 is the EU system for the entry summary declaration (ENS) of goods coming from outside the customs security area. On the intra-area EU-Norway route the usual are the Digitoll messages, the EU export declaration and the Norwegian import declaration.

EU phytosanitary controls in 2026: inspection frequencies

The new frequencies of identity and physical controls of plants and plant products apply from 11 June 2026. The concept of 100 % base frequency revised according to risk and the increase for avocado from all non-EU countries is explained.

Peruvian fruit to the EU: SENASA certificate, MRL and traceability

Conceptual guide of the usual requirements for Peruvian fruit destined for the EU: SENASA phytosanitary certificate, compliance with EU MRL, labelling and traceability to packing and producer. It is recalled that a shipment to Norway is not the same as to the EU.

Avocado from Peru: more identity and physical controls in the EU

Specific article on avocado from Peru and the increase in identity and physical controls in the EU from 11 June 2026 according to AGRINFO. It focuses on documentary preparation and lot quality, without forecasting rejections or inventing interception rates.

Norwegian salmon export: how to read the official SSB statistics

Explanation of the official SSB figures on export of fresh/chilled and frozen farmed salmon. Only the data from the week 45 of 2026 table are used and what the statistical FOB value according to SSB is clarified. Without forecasts or purchase or sale recommendations.

Cold chain and risk transfer in fresh foods

The maintenance of the cold chain is combined with the moment when risk is transferred according to the Incoterms. The term does not “follow the temperature”; a cold break falls on whoever bears the risk at that moment.

DUIMP in Brazil (2026): the move to pre-validated data in import

Description of the move from a border correction model to a pre-validated data model in Brazilian import. Useful for European or Norwegian exporters selling to Brazil and for readers in Latin America.

Landed cost: usual components, without invented tariffs

The usual components of the landed cost are described without inventing freights, duties or taxes. Reference is made to the articles on Incoterms, HS classification and import VAT/MVA in Norway.

Intermediary versus importer of record

Explains in general terms the difference between an intermediary or commercial agent acting by mandate and the figure of the importer or exporter of record responsible before customs. Fits with intermediation models from Oslo in which clearance is carried out through an authorized customs representative.

How an international sales mandate is built

Describes, as an educational process, what elements are usually clarified before starting the search for counterparties in an international sales mandate: goods, market, scope, documentation and limits. Sober tone, without promises of results.

Sea, air or road: criteria for choosing the mode

Explains conceptual criteria for choosing between maritime, air or road transport: nature of the goods, deadline, cold chain and documents specific to each mode. Reminds that Norway is outside the EU customs union.

Letter of credit and documentary collections

Educational article on the usual documentary instruments: letter of credit or documentary credit and documentary collection. Mentions the ICC UCP 600 when the credit incorporates them.

Electronic invoice in Norway: EHF and Peppol BIS 3.0

Describes the electronic invoicing regulation adopted on 29 September 2026 by the Norwegian tax administration. Obligation to issue electronic invoices between accounting subjects from 1 January 2027 in EHF and Peppol BIS 3.0 or later formats.

Advance submission of documents by API to Tollvesenet

Explains the possibility of sending in advance, via API, supporting documents for the declaration (invoices, permits, freights, certificates of origin, bills of lading) so that they are available before the arrival of the goods. The customs describes it as a solution in pilot phase.

eFTI and electronic transport information

Explains the EU eFTI framework, which allows presenting certain transport information electronically through certified platforms. As of November 2026 Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway were still examining its incorporation into the EEA Agreement, so it still does not apply in Norway.

Commercial invoice, packing list and customs coherence

Goes deeper into the necessary coherence between the commercial invoice, the packing list and the customs declaration (description, quantities, value, Incoterm, origin, HS code). Links with the quality of the master data and with Digitoll.

VAT/MVA on importation to Norway

Explains that companies registered in the MVA register calculate the import VAT themselves and declare it in the mva-melding before Skatteetaten. The base is, as a general rule, the statistical value (box 46) plus duties and other levies (box 47).

Due diligence on the counterparty and the supplier

Educational article on the usual checks before signing with an international counterparty: company identity, coherence of the documentation, licenses or certificates required by the goods and clarity of the mandate and Incoterms.
