eFTI and electronic transport information

Introducción

Regulation (EU) 2020/1056 on electronic freight transport information (eFTI) establishes a framework so that certain transport information can be presented electronically to the authorities through certified platforms. As of November 2026 the regulation does not yet apply in Norway because Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway were still examining its incorporation into the Agreement on the European Economic Area.

This article describes what eFTI is and what it is not. It must not be confused with Digitoll.

Qué es el Reglamento eFTI

eFTI allows operators to make available to the competent authorities certain regulatory transport information in electronic format, instead of on paper, through platforms that meet the requirements of the regulation. The authorities of the EU Member States shall accept that information from the provided dates (the framework points to July 2027 for full acceptance by the authorities).

Alcance y plataformas certificadas

The use by companies is, in principle, voluntary; the main obligation falls on the authorities to accept the information when it is presented through the system. The regulation mainly covers transport by road, rail, inland waterways and, in part, air, with exclusions and nuances according to the implementing acts.

Situación en Noruega a noviembre de 2026

According to information from Statens vegvesen and sector references, eFTI was not part of the EEA Agreement in mid-2026. If it is not incorporated before the dates of full application in the EU, the entry into force in Norway would be delayed. Therefore, as of 14 November 2026 it cannot be stated that eFTI is mandatory or applicable in Norway.

Incorporación al Acuerdo EEE pendiente

The incorporation of EU acts into the EEA Agreement requires a decision of the Joint Committee. Until that incorporation takes place and enters into force, the eFTI framework does not generate obligations in Norwegian territory. It may, however, affect shipments directed to or coming from the EU when the authorities of the Member States require or accept electronic information.

Diferencia con Digitoll

Digitoll is the Norwegian system of digital notification and advance declaration before Tollvesenet. eFTI is an EU framework for transport information before control authorities. They are different instruments, with different legal bases and calendars. They must not be used as synonyms.

Descargo

Informative text as of November 2026. It does not state that eFTI is mandatory in Norway. The situation of incorporation into the EEA Agreement may evolve; consult official sources of the EU, the EEA and the Norwegian authorities.

Is eFTI mandatory in Norway?
Not as of November 2026. Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein were still examining its incorporation into the EEA Agreement.
Does eFTI replace Digitoll?
No. They are different frameworks. Digitoll is the Norwegian system of notification and advance declaration.
Can it affect shipments towards the EU?
Yes, to the extent that the authorities of the EU Member States must accept electronic information from the dates provided in the regulation.
