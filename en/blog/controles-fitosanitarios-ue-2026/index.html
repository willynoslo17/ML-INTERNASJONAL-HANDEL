EU phytosanitary controls in 2026: inspection frequencies

Introducción

The European Union periodically reviews the frequencies of the identity and physical controls of shipments of plants, plant products and other objects that enter the Union. According to AGRINFO (report “Frequency rates for plant import controls 2026”, published on 29 May 2026), the new frequencies apply from 11 June 2026, by virtue of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1101 of 22 May 2026 which amends Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/2389.

This article explains the concept of base frequency and the changes that the source highlights, in particular the increase for avocado. It does not invent inspection percentages or forecast rejections.

Frecuencia base y revisión según riesgo

Desde el 11 de junio de 2026

For shipments of plants and plant products that enter the EU, the base frequency of identity and physical controls is 100 % of all shipments. However, a lower frequency can be established for specific plants. These frequencies are reviewed regularly according to phytosanitary risk.

The review takes into account the biology of the pests (especially the potential mobility of quarantine pests), the compliance records, previous interceptions and trade volumes. The information comes from IMSOC, TRACES and the Member States.

The new frequencies resulting from the 2026 review apply from 11 June 2026.

Aumento de controles para el aguacate

Persea americana de todos los países no UE

AGRINFO indicates an increase in the identity and physical controls of avocado (*Persea americana*) coming from all countries not belonging to the EU. Increases are also mentioned for citrus from Mexico, *Prunus* from European countries outside the EU, roots and tubers of turmeric from all non-EU countries (with higher frequency for Thailand and Peru) and ginger from Peru, among others.

At the same time, the source records decreases for other products and origins (for example, apples, pears and blueberries from European countries outside the EU, citrus from Turkey, etc.). The exact details of each product-origin combination are found in the regulation and in the accompanying tables. This article does not reproduce percentages that are not explicitly in the source consulted.

Cómo se determina la frecuencia

Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 on plant health and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/2389 establish the framework. High-risk products remain subject to 100 % controls. The reduced frequency is a risk-based facilitation measure and may be revised upwards if interceptions increase or the risk profile changes.

For exporters from non-EU countries, preparation includes the phytosanitary certificate of the country of origin, compliance with EU requirements (among them Regulation (EU) 2019/2072 on plant health) and the quality and traceability of the lot. An increase in the frequency of controls implies that a higher percentage of shipments may be selected for identity or physical inspection at the border control point.

Descargo

Information based on AGRINFO and on the regulations cited. It does not constitute personalised phytosanitary or customs advice. The exact frequencies and the requirements of each shipment must be verified in the official sources in force and with the competent authorities of the country of destination.

From when do the new frequencies apply?
According to AGRINFO, the new frequencies of identity and physical controls apply from 11 June 2026 (Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1101).
What is the base frequency?
The base frequency of identity and physical controls is 100 % of the shipments. A lower frequency can be established for specific plants and is reviewed periodically according to risk.
What happens with avocado?
AGRINFO indicates an increase in the identity and physical controls of avocado (Persea americana) coming from all countries not belonging to the EU.
Are concrete percentages invented?
No. Only the changes that appear in the AGRINFO source are cited. The exact percentages of each origin must be consulted in the regulation and in the official tables.
