ICS2: when a security notification applies

Introducción

ICS2 (Import Control System 2) is the system of the European Union for the electronic submission of the entry summary declaration (Entry Summary Declaration, ENS) for security and safety purposes. According to the European Commission and Tolletaten, all goods that enter or transit the EU customs security area from third countries must be pre-notified in ICS2 so that the authorities carry out the risk analysis before arrival.

This article explains the concept and the difference with Digitoll, especially in the EU-Norway corridor. It does not indicate whether a concrete shipment must submit ICS2 or offer templates.

Qué es ICS2 de forma conceptual

Declaración sumaria de entrada (ENS)

The ENS contains security data: description of the goods (HS code to 6 digits), parties involved, transport data and means of transport. The authorities carry out a risk analysis prior to loading or arrival. Depending on the result they may request additional information or, in exceptional cases, issue recommendations not to load.

Submission deadlines vary according to the mode: by road, sources cite at least one hour before arrival at the first point of entry. The system has been implemented in phases; since April 2025 it is available for all modes of transport, with transition periods that have already concluded according to the publications consulted.

Cuándo se solicita la notificación de seguridad

Diferencia con Digitoll

ICS2 applies to goods that come from outside the EU customs security area. Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Switzerland form part of that area (customs security agreement in force since 1 July 2009, incorporated into the EEA). Therefore, movements between the EU and Norway do not require, in principle, the ICS2 ENS for goods that are already within the area.

Digitoll is the Norwegian system of notification and digital disclosure of transport and goods before or, at the latest, at the crossing of the Norwegian border. According to sources from Tolletaten and logistics operators, from 15 September 2026 the obligation of notification and information is digital and prior. The customs declaration proper (in TVINN or NCTS) has its own calendar (forecast of submission at the latest at the crossing from 1 March 2027).

A logistics source (RNJ Transport) indicates that, for a truck with goods from the EU to Norway, the ICS2 security notification is requested when the goods come from outside the area; on the intra-area route the usual are the Digitoll messages, the EU export declaration and the Norwegian import declaration.

Trayecto camión de la UE hacia Noruega

In practice, a truck that loads in an EU country and heads to Norway presents, on the one hand, the export declaration in the EU and, on the other, the Digitoll messages to Tolletaten, in addition to the Norwegian import declaration when applicable. If the goods have previously entered the security area from a third country, the ICS2 ENS will already have been submitted at the first point of entry into the EU.

No nuance is asserted here that is not supported by the sources cited. Coordination between the carrier, the shipper and the authorised customs representative is essential so that the messages are correctly linked.

Descargo

General information based on public sources. It does not constitute personalised customs advice. The need to submit ICS2, Digitoll or any other declaration depends on the concrete origin of the goods, the mode of transport and the regulation in force at the time of the operation. Consult an authorised customs representative.

What is ICS2?
It is the EU system for the security and safety pre-arrival of goods that enter or transit the EU customs security area. An Entry Summary Declaration (ENS) is submitted.
Does ICS2 apply to a truck leaving the EU for Norway?
Norway has been part of the EU customs security area since 2009. According to logistics sources and Tolletaten, the ICS2 notification is requested when the goods come from outside that area. On the intra-area route the usual are the Digitoll messages.
What difference is there between ICS2 and Digitoll?
ICS2 is the EU security pre-arrival for third countries. Digitoll is the Norwegian system of notification and digital information before or at the crossing of the Norwegian border.
Who submits the ENS?
The economic operator who brings the goods in. The deadlines depend on the mode of transport (for example, one hour before arrival by road according to sources).
