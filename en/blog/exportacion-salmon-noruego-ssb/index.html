Norwegian salmon export: how to read the official SSB statistics

Introducción

Statistics Norway (SSB) publishes weekly the export statistics of fresh/chilled and frozen farmed salmon. The update of 11 November 2026 corresponds to week 45 of 2026 (period from 2 to 8 November). This article explains how to read those figures: average price per kilo, quantity in tonnes and the concept of statistical FOB value according to SSB. No price forecasts are made nor is buying or selling recommended.

What SSB publishes and with what frequency

Week 45 of 2026

SSB updates the table every week. In the publication of 11 November 2026 the following are indicated, among other data:

  • Price per kg (NOK) of fresh or chilled salmon: 73,35 (variation −2,2 % compared to the previous week and −6,1 % compared to the same week of 2025).
  • Quantity (tonnes) of fresh or chilled salmon: 28 277 (−0,6 % compared to the previous week and +17,3 % compared to the same week of 2025).
  • Price per kg of frozen salmon: 75,12.
  • Quantity of frozen salmon: 868 tonnes.

These are the only numbers reproduced here, taken directly from the SSB table of that date. The selected commodity codes are 03.02.1411 and 03.02.1419 for fresh and 03.03.1311 and 03.03.1319 for frozen.

Average price, quantity and statistical FOB value

What the SSB FOB value includes and what it does not include

The average price is the average export price per kilo of that week. The quantity is the net mass in tonnes.

The statistical value, according to the SSB definition, is the value when crossing the Norwegian border, including transport and insurance costs. It is also called FOB value (Free On Board). It does not include customs duties, VAT or other taxes.

This definition is useful to understand what the value figure represents in Norway’s foreign trade statistics. It is not the final selling price to the consumer nor does it include costs after the destination border.

How to use the table without making forecasts

The weekly figures allow observation of the recent evolution of the volume and the average export price. Comparing the current week with the previous one and with the same week of the previous year provides context, but does not constitute a forecast. Factors such as farmed supply, demand in destination markets, the exchange rate and logistical conditions influence the figures, but this article does not analyse or project them.

For companies that export or import Norwegian salmon, the SSB statistics are an official reference source on the volume and statistical value at the Norwegian border. The cold chain, Incoterms and landed costs are related topics treated in other articles of this series.

Descargo

Figures taken exclusively from the SSB table of week 45 of 2026 (update 11 November 2026). It does not constitute commercial advice or a price forecast. Companies must consult the updated official sources and their advisers for concrete decisions.

Where do the figures come from?
From the official SSB statistic «Export of salmon», update of 11 November 2026, table of weekly exports of farmed salmon.
What is the average price per kilo?
The average export price per kilo of the week, in Norwegian kroner, for fresh/chilled and for frozen separately.
What is the statistical FOB value according to SSB?
The value when crossing the Norwegian border, including transport and insurance. It does not include duties or VAT.
Are price forecasts made?
No. This article only explains how to read the published official figures.
