Commercial invoice, packing list and customs coherence

Introducción

The commercial invoice and the packing list are central documents in any import or export operation. They must be coherent with each other and with the customs declaration. This article describes, in general terms, what information each usually contains and why the coincidence of data is important.

It does not offer downloadable models or forms that claim to be official.

Función de la factura comercial

The commercial invoice documents the purchase and sale operation: parties, description of the goods, quantities, price, currency, Incoterm and payment conditions. It serves as a basis for the customs valuation and for other controls.

Datos que suelen figurar

In addition to the data of the parties and the description, it usually includes the declared origin, the order references and, when applicable, the HS code used by the parties. Those elements must be reconcilable with the packing list and with what is declared.

Función del packing list

The packing list details how the goods are packed: number of packages, content of each one, gross and net weights, and marks. It facilitates the physical inspection and the verification of quantities.

Relación con bultos y cantidades

The total quantities of the packing list must coincide with those of the invoice. The descriptions must be compatible. Any difference (for example, an additional package not invoiced or a more generic description) may generate questions.

Coherencia con la declaración aduanera

The customs declaration takes data from these documents and from the company's master data: description, quantities, statistical value, Incoterm, origin and HS code. The coherence between invoice, packing list, transport document and declaration reduces the risk of additional controls.

In the Norwegian context, the possibility of sending documents in advance via the Tollvesenet API makes it even more relevant that the information is aligned from the origin. Well-maintained master data (stable descriptions, reviewed HS codes, documented origins) facilitate that coherence.

Descargo

Informative text. It does not constitute a model of invoice or packing list nor customs advice. The accuracy of the declaration is the responsibility of the declarant or of its authorized representative. Consult official and professional sources.

Is the commercial invoice the same as the electronic tax invoice?
Not necessarily. The commercial invoice serves for the trade and customs operation; the electronic tax invoice responds to local accounting and tax requirements.
What happens if there are discrepancies?
They may generate queries from the customs, delays or need for rectification. Coherence reduces that risk.
Does the packing list replace the invoice?
No. It complements the invoice by detailing the content of the packages.
