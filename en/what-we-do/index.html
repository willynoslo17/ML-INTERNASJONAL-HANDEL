What we do

We manage the full cycle of an international commercial transaction: from defining the need to confirming delivery.

Process

  • Definition of requirements and conditions.
  • Search and evaluation of counterparts.
  • Presentation and verification of match.
  • Support in negotiation and closing of terms.
  • Coordination of documentation and logistics.
  • Follow-up until receipt and closure.
